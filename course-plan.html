Create a Course Plan

Already have a course plan? Set the start date and class days below, then press Upload my plan and choose your Excel, PDF or Word file — in your own format. The app reads the weeks and topics, shows them to you to check, and turns them into a tick-off checklist. No file? Press Create Plan for an empty week-by-week skeleton, or Paste a list of topics.

Planned vs. Actually Covered

Paste your topics

Copy the topics from anywhere (Word, PDF, WhatsApp, a website) and paste them here — one topic per line. Lines like "Week 3" start a new week; without them the topics are spread over your class days.
